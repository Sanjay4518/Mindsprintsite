Why MindSprint

Built for how you actually revise

Short, focused sessions that fit between classes, commutes, or study breaks — with a progression system that keeps you coming back.

Three Ways to Play

Normal Mode for steady, category-focused practice, Rapid Fire for a 90-second speed test, and Practice Mode to keep revisiting only the questions you've gotten wrong, untimed, until you've truly mastered them.

XP Leagues

Focused Practice
